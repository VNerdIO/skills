# Food City aisle map

Sections in **walking order** for a typical Food City: produce at the entrance, deli
and bakery nearby, center aisles numbered from one side, meat and dairy along the back
and side walls. Layouts vary a lot between older and newer stores. Many Food City
prices in the weekly ad require the ValuCard ("With Card").
